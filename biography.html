Academic Biography

Curriculum vitae narrative, research background, academic appointments, and public service record.

Behrouz Minaei-Bidgoli is a Full Professor in the School of Computer Engineering at Iran University of Science and Technology (IUST), Tehran, Iran, and the founder and director of the Data Mining Laboratory (DML). His research encompasses natural language processing, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), knowledge graph construction and reasoning, and educational data mining.

According to Google Scholar, he has authored and co-authored over 440 refereed journal articles, conference papers, and book chapters, receiving more than 9,800 citations with an h-index of 51. He has supervised or co-supervised over 60 graduate theses (Ph.D. and M.Sc.) at IUST and collaborating universities.

2. Academic Appointments & University Leadership

Following the completion of his doctorate at Michigan State University in 2004, Minaei returned to Iran and joined the School of Computer Engineering at IUST in 2005, advancing to the rank of Full Professor.

Director, Data Mining Laboratory (DML)
2005 – Present • IUST

Founded DML as an active university laboratory conducting research in natural language processing, semantic web, recommender algorithms, and educational analytics.

Dean, School of Computer Engineering

Supervised departmental administration, curriculum updates across undergraduate and postgraduate degrees, and university research facilities.

3. National Language Technology & Public Service

Alongside academic appointments, Prof. Minaei served in several national information technology and digital media directorships:

Technical Deputy • Secretariat of the Supreme Council of Information

Oversaw national research initiatives in Persian language computing. Directed the development of Virastyar, a widely adopted open Persian spellchecker, text normalizer, and grammar engine for word processing environments. Coordinated the Persian Font Working Group for digital Nastaliq typography standardization, and sponsored joint projects in statistical machine translation, speech recognition, and Persian OCR.

Founding Director & CEO • Iran Computer and Video Games Foundation (2007 – 2013)

Led the establishment of the national foundation supporting domestic game software engineering. Established the Entertainment Software Rating Association (ESRA) age-classification framework, founded the Game Development Institute, and cooperated with higher education bodies to introduce academic programs in game design and development.

4. Research Focus & Laboratory Directions

Current investigations at the Data Mining Laboratory center on the intersection of Large Language Models, structured knowledge, and reliability:

Reasoning & Faithful RAG

Mitigating hallucinations and improving reasoning capabilities in Large Language Models using prompt optimization, preference alignment, and graph-augmented retrieval.

Knowledge Graphs (FarsBase)

Constructing and completing multi-modal semantic knowledge bases. Developing entity alignment, link prediction, and consistency verification frameworks in the Semantic Web.

Trustworthy Machine Learning

Addressing uncertainty estimation, robustness under distribution shift, and verifiable decision-making in deep learning architectures.

Computational Linguistics & Corpora

Developing specialized text processing architectures, sentiment benchmarks, and legal/hadith analytical tools for Persian and Arabic historical texts.

5. Biographical Notes & Career Context

Born in 1962 in Bidgol, Iran, Minaei completed his primary and secondary education in Kashan before entering university studies in mathematics. During the Iran-Iraq War (1980–1988), he served as a volunteer on critical frontlines.

During the early 1990s, alongside his university mathematics training, he worked as Software Director at the Computer Research Center of Islamic Sciences (Noor Center). There, he wrote low-level Assembly and C text-processing routines and search engines for large-scale digitized classical Arabic and Persian historical encyclopedias, contributing to the establishment of digital humanities infrastructure in the country.

In 1999, he relocated to the United States to pursue his doctoral degree at Michigan State University. His work with the LON-CAPA research group resulted in several early publications on genetic algorithms and machine learning for educational systems. Upon completing his Ph.D. in 2004, he chose to return to Iran to develop postgraduate data engineering programs, establish DML, and advise emerging researchers.
