I am a Full Professor in the School of Computer Engineering at Iran University of Science and Technology (IUST) in Tehran, Iran. I previously served as the Dean of the School of Computer Engineering and Director of Research for the department.

I direct the Data Mining Laboratory (DML) (DML LinkedIn) at IUST. My research interests span Natural Language Processing & Faithful RAG, Knowledge Graph Construction (FarsBase), Trustworthy Machine Learning, Recommender Systems, and Educational Data Mining.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science & Engineering from Michigan State University (MSU) in 2004 under Dr. William F. Punch, focusing on feature extraction and sequence mining in online educational systems (LON-CAPA). I also served as founding CEO of the Iran Computer and Video Games Foundation (2007–2013) and Technical Deputy of the Secretariat of the Supreme Council of Information, directing Persian language computing initiatives including the Virastyar software project.
