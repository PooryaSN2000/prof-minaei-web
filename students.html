Doctoral Alumni Career Placements

Graduates of our laboratory hold faculty, research scientist, and technical leadership positions worldwide:

  • Academic Institutions: Faculty members and postdoctoral researchers at top universities in Iran, Europe, and North America.
  • Global Research & Industry: AI research scientists and senior engineers at technology enterprises and research laboratories (EPFL, Max Planck Institute, University of Toronto, UBC, etc.).
  • National Technology Initiatives: Leadership roles across data engineering, NLP software systems, and interactive digital media.

Information for Prospective Advisees

I welcome motivated graduate students (Ph.D. and M.Sc.) who are eager to learn and explore research in artificial intelligence, machine learning, and data mining.

If you are interested in conducting your thesis research at DML, please send an email with your CV (PDF format) and a brief note about your background and interests to b_minaei@iust.ac.ir with the subject format [DML Prospective Student] [Ph.D. / M.Sc.] Your Full Name – Research Area.
